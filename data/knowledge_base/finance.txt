FINANCE KNOWLEDGE BASE

Fee and Payment:
Verify the student's payment details, transaction reference, fee record, and payment status.

Billing Issues:
Check the billing record and compare the reported amount with the official fee information.

Refund Request:
Verify the original transaction, refund eligibility, and required approval before processing a refund.

Scholarship and Financial Aid:
Verify the student's financial-aid record and supporting information before escalating the request.
