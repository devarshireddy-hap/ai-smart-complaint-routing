SECURITY KNOWLEDGE BASE

Theft or Loss:
Record the location, approximate time, and description of the lost or stolen item and notify security personnel.

Safety Concern:
Record the location and nature of the safety concern and escalate urgent situations immediately.

Access Issue:
Verify the affected access point and determine whether the issue involves authorization, access control equipment, or a security procedure.

Suspicious Activity:
Record the location, time, and observed activity and notify the security team for investigation.
