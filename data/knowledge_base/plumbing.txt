PLUMBING KNOWLEDGE BASE

Water Leakage:
For a reported water leakage, identify the exact location and estimate whether the leakage is minor or severe.

Minor leakage:
Inspect the pipe, valve, tap, or fitting and schedule maintenance.

Severe leakage:
Notify the plumbing maintenance team immediately and restrict the affected area if necessary.

No Water Supply:
Check the reported building, floor, washroom, or water line and verify whether the issue affects one location or multiple locations.

Blocked Drain:
Inspect the affected drain and remove the blockage. If the blockage cannot be resolved locally, assign the issue to the plumbing maintenance team.

Washroom Plumbing:
For washroom-related plumbing complaints, inspect water supply, drainage, taps, pipes, and fixtures.
